# FAQ
Here is a list of some of the most common questions about Cubyz.

## Gameplay Questions

### How do I play the game?
See the [Installation Guide](/installation.html) for details.

### I'm on MacOS, can I play?
Cubyz does not support MacOS as of 0.4.0, though progress towards MacOS support is being made through the Vulkan rewrite.

### Game doesn't start, what do I do?
You can create an issue on [GitHub](https://github.com/PixelGuys/Cubyz/issues), or ask for help in the [Cubyz Discord's](https://discord.gg/8HhcnDUV) help forum.

### Why is my game lagging? 
The LOD1 Distance has the greatest performance impact. Lowering it can improve performance. You can also try changing the Highest LOD or Resolution Scale if needed.

If you are experiencing lag while around forests, try adjusting the Leaves Quality and Opaque Leaves Distance.

### How do I heal? 
The only way to regain your health is to die.

### How do I eat food? 
You don't. The stomach "energy bar" does not do anything.

### How do I set my spawn point?
Your spawn point is only able to be set by the `/spawn` command.

### I want to contribute, where can I start? 
Cubyz relies heavily on community support, and there are many ways to help! You can check out our dedicated [How to Contribute](development/how_to_contribute.md) page for full details. 

* **For Code:** Read the [Contribution Guidelines](https://github.com/PixelGuys/Cubyz/blob/master/docs/CONTRIBUTING.md) and look for open issues on GitHub.
* **For Content Additions:** Read the [Content Suggestion Guidelines](https://github.com/PixelGuys/Cubyz/blob/master/docs/CONTENT_SUGGESTIONS.md).
* **For Community Roles:** You can apply to join our YouTube, Discord, Wiki, GitHub, or Reddit teams.

If you have any questions or just want to chat with the team, feel free to ask in our [Discord](https://discord.gg/8HhcnDUV).
